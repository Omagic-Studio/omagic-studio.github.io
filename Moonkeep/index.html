About the Game

Moonkeep is a merge tower defense game on a small island. By day you build towers on a 5×5 board and drag two towers of the same level together to merge them into a stronger one. When night falls, bats, crows and wraiths swoop down from the sky and your towers fire on their own. Every monster that slips past costs a heart — how many nights can you hold?

How to Play

  1. By day, tap Build to place a level 1 tower on a free tile, and spend gold to unlock more tiles.
  2. Drag two towers of the same level together to merge them into the next level.
  3. At night your towers fire on their own. Stop the monsters before they reach the bottom edge — each one that gets past costs a heart.
