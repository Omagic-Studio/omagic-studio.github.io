Privacy Policy

Moonkeep: Merge Tower Defense
Omagic Studio  ·  Last updated: November 25, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Moonkeep: Merge Tower Defense (the "Game"), a mobile tower defense game by Omagic Studio ("we", "us"). In short: the Game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or contacts. Your progress is saved only on your device. The Game shows ads from Google AdMob, offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play and uses Google Firebase for usage statistics and crash reports; Google and its ad partners may collect device information as described below.
🎮

Information the Game Stores on Your Device

Your current run (day, board, towers, gold, hearts), records and top-10 scores, tower handbook, skins and purchases you made, and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are saved in files on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

📢

Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the main screen, a full-screen ad when a run ends, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to unlock a tile, revive once per run or double a boss reward).

To deliver and measure ads, Google and the ad networks it works with may collect:

  • Device identifiers – the Android advertising ID and similar identifiers
  • Network and device information – IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device model, operating system, language
  • Ad interactions – which ads were shown, watched or tapped
  • Diagnostics – ad loading performance and errors

Ad partners that may serve ads through AdMob include Unity Ads and Liftoff (Vungle). Their use of data is governed by their own policies: Google, Unity, Liftoff.

🛒

In-App Purchases

You can buy optional items (Remove Ads, tower skins, island skins, starter pack). Purchases only change how the Game looks or remove ads; they never sell gold, hearts or power. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your payment details. The Game keeps a record of the items you bought on your device so they are not delivered twice, and "Restore purchases" asks Google Play for your past purchases.

📊

Analytics and Crash Reports

The Game uses Google Firebase to understand how it is played and to fix bugs:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) – usage statistics such as app opens, session length, screens viewed and in-app purchase events, together with an app instance identifier, device model, operating system, language and approximate location (from the IP address).
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) – crash reports with device and app state at the time of the crash.

This data does not identify you by name and is used only to improve the Game. Where consent is required, analytics follows the choice you make in the consent form (see below). See Firebase Privacy and Security.

✅

Your Choices and Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before personalized ads are shown and before analytics data is collected. Where US state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data in the same way. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button in the Game's Settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

👶

Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

🔒

Security and Retention

We do not run servers that store your data. Information collected by Google (AdMob, Firebase) and its ad partners is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to their policies; for example, Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

🗑️

Deleting Your Data

Uninstall the Game to delete everything it saved on your device. To ask about data held by Google, ad partners or about your purchase history, use the links above, your Google Play account, or contact us and we will help.

🔄

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
