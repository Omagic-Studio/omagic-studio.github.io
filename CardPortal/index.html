♠ About the Game

Four classic card games in one calm, easy-to-read app. Sit down at a warm card-club table with hand-painted cards and friendly computer opponents, and play Gin Rummy, Rummy 500, Spades or Tonk whenever you like, with or without an internet connection.

Coins in the game are just for fun: they are virtual, have no cash value and cannot be bought or cashed out. The game does not offer real-money gambling.

Features

🃏

Four classic games

Gin Rummy, Rummy 500, Spades (Classic partners or Solo) and Tonk, with standard rules and scoring.

🔍

Easy on the eyes

Large cards and buttons, three text sizes and an optional 4-color deck.

🐢

Your pace

Relaxed, Normal or Fast game speed. No turn timer, only an optional gentle reminder.

📖

Learn as you play

Illustrated rules, first-hand tips, tap any term for a quick explanation, and undo your last discard.

🤝

Friendly computer opponents

Three levels: Easy, Medium and Hard. Each opponent has a name, a face and a playing style.

📶

Play offline

No Wi-Fi needed. Your progress is saved on your device.

How to Play

  1. Pick a game in the lobby: Gin Rummy, Rummy 500, Spades or Tonk.
  2. Choose a table and a difficulty: Easy, Medium or Hard.
  3. Tap "?" at any time for illustrated rules, then deal and enjoy.
