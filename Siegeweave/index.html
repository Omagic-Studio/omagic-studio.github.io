About the Game

Tiny toy armies fight across a handcrafted felt board. Every battle lasts about 3 minutes, plays with one hand and works offline.

Bend the enemy's path with palisades, send squads to capture neutral towers, and crack the rival Core before the clock runs out.

How to Play

  1. Tap one of your towers, then tap a target to send a squad.
  2. Drag palisades on the road to make enemy squads take the long way round.
  3. Capture towers and break the enemy Core before the clock runs out.
