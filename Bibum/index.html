🐾 About the Game

Aim your trajectory, let go, and unleash an avalanche of bouncing balls! BiBum takes classic brick breaker gameplay to new heights with smooth physics, explosive combos and heartwarming pet rescue adventures. Watch the numbers tick down to zero with every ricochet.

  2. Release to launch a swarm of balls.
  3. Break every brick before they reach the bottom.
