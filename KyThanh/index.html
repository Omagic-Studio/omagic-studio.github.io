♟️ About the Game

KyThanh is a Chinese Chess (Xiangqi) game inspired by Vietnamese chess traditions. Discover three ways to play in one app: classic Cờ Tướng, hidden-piece Cờ Úp and Cờ Thế endgame puzzles. Plan your attack, protect your General and take control of the board.

How to Play

  1. Move each piece by its own rule.
  2. Protect your General and watch for threats.
  3. Checkmate the opposing General to win.
