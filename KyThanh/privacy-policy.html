Information We Collect

Information you provide

The Service does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The Service does not offer in-app purchases.

Your game progress, puzzle progress and settings are saved locally on your Device. We do not upload them to our servers. You can erase them by uninstalling the Service.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, the Third-Party Services listed below may automatically collect:

  • Device and app information – device model, operating system version, language, app version and network type.

  • Advertising data – which ads are shown and whether you view or tap them.
  • Usage data – app events such as screens opened and games played.
  • Diagnostic data – crash logs and performance information when the app stops unexpectedly.
  • Notification token – a token that allows the Service to receive push notifications, if you allow notifications.

The Service displays banner ads and interstitial ads between some games. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your choices and the laws of your region.

You can turn off push notifications at any time in your Device settings.
