About the Game

Tap a cord and it slides out the way its arrow points. Clear every cord in the right order and a hand-painted picture appears beneath them.

Knot Gallery is a calm logic puzzle with braided cords, simple rules and no timer. Four chapters of illustrated Vietnamese folk pictures wait in your Picture Album.

How to Play

  1. Tap a cord: it slides out the way its arrow points.
  2. If something blocks the path, the cord bounces back and you lose a heart.
  3. Clear every cord to reveal the picture and add it to your album.
