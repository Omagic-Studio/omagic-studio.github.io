✈️ About the Game

Propstorm is an offline vertical sky shooter built around quick movement, readable bullet patterns and boss fights that reward a well-timed dodge. Weave between incoming fire, unleash your special ability and break through the next wave.

Features

🗺️

60 campaign stages

Six regions, from coral islands to a fortress above the clouds.

👾

Multi-phase bosses

Break apart turrets, wings and engines on huge bosses.

🛩️

Unique aircraft

Each plane has its own weapons and special ability.

🤝

Wingmen & Hangar

Bring wingmen into battle and upgrade your firepower.

📅

Daily Sortie

A fresh stage every day, plus Survival and Training modes.

📶

Play offline

No connection needed. Three difficulty levels for every stage.

How to Play

  1. Drag to move your plane, it fires automatically.
  2. Weave through bullet patterns and collect upgrades.
  3. Use your special ability to break through bosses.
