About the Game

The Watch Lanterns have gone dark. Only one flame is left, and you carry it.

Wickkeeper is a cozy-spooky survival roguelite: walk into the Misty Cemetery, push back waves of ghosts with weapons that fire on their own, and keep your flame alive until dawn. No blood and no gore.

Features

🕯

One-thumb action

Drag to move. Weapons fire on their own. Ghosts slow down inside your light.

⚔

New build every run

12 weapons, 10 passive charms and weapon evolutions such as Sea of Fire and Sun Mirror.

👻

Spooky, not scary

Hand-painted ghosts, skeletons and crows. Defeated ghosts turn into freed souls.

🏘

The Last Village

Spend gold at the Forge, Inn, Chapel and Oil Shop for 10 permanent upgrades.

🧙

Three heroes

The Lamplighter, The Bell Keeper and The Firefly Boy, each with a unique bonus.

🌏

5 languages

English, Tiếng Việt, Deutsch, Español and Português (Brasil).

How to Play

  1. Drag to move; your weapons attack on their own.
  2. Gather freed souls to level up and pick one of three cards.
  3. Keep your flame alive until dawn, then upgrade the village for the next run.
