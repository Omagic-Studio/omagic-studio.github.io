Privacy Policy

Hush Mouse
Omagic Studio  ·  Last updated: November 22, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Hush Mouse (the "Game"), a mobile game by Omagic Studio ("we", "us"). In short: the Game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or contacts. Your progress is saved only on your device. The Game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google and its ad partners may collect device information to show those ads, as described below.
🎮

Information the Game Stores on Your Device

Your level progress and settings (sound, music, language, tutorials seen) are saved in files on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

📢

Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are optional: a rewarded video is only shown when you choose to watch one (for example to rewind time after being caught).

To deliver and measure ads, Google and the ad networks it works with may collect:

  • Device identifiers – the Android advertising ID and similar identifiers
  • Network and device information – IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device model, operating system, language
  • Ad interactions – which ads were shown, watched or tapped
  • Diagnostics – ad loading performance and errors

Ad partners that may serve ads through AdMob include Unity Ads and Liftoff (Vungle). Their use of data is governed by their own policies: Google, Unity, Liftoff.

✅

Your Choices and Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before personalized ads are shown. Where US state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data in the same way. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy settings button in the Game's Settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

👶

Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

🔒

Security and Retention

We do not run servers that store your data. Information collected by Google and its ad partners is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to their policies.

🗑️

Deleting Your Data

Uninstall the Game to delete everything it saved on your device. To ask about data held by ad partners, use the links above or contact us and we will help.

🔄

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
