About the Game

Shh... The cats of the Cat Castle are on guard tonight. Tiptoe through the shadows, creep up behind each cat and sprinkle a pinch of sleep dust. Yawn... zzz!

Hush Mouse is a cozy cat and mouse stealth puzzle you play with one thumb: a game of hide and seek in a castle full of cat guards. Nobody gets hurt. Cats only ever fall asleep, and if you are caught, a teacup drops over you.

Features

😴

Sprinkle sleep dust

Creep up behind each cat and sprinkle a pinch of sleep dust. Nobody gets hurt: cats only ever fall asleep.

🕯️

Stay in the dark

Every cat has a vision cone. Blow out candles, hide under teacups and behind stacks of books.

🧶

Outsmart the guards

Throw yarn balls to send guards the wrong way, or dive into a mouse hole and pop out of its twin.

🏰

300 levels, 5 zones

Kitchen Pantry, Library, Night Garden, Snowy Attic and Throne Hall, with King Cat boss levels.

🐱

Know your cats

Guards, patrol cats, hawk eyes, wide eyes, and playful kittens that squeal if they spot you.

🌍

2 languages

English and Tiếng Việt, in a handmade paper world lit by candlelight.

How to Play

  1. Drag below the map to move the mouse. Watch each cat's vision cone and slip past behind it.
  2. Creep up behind a cat and sprinkle sleep dust to put it to sleep.
  3. Complete the level's mission: put every cat to sleep, grab the cheese and escape, or free a friend from a mousetrap.
