About the Game

Left at the altar, Ava Bell has one place to go: home. In the seaside town of Bayhaven, her grandmother Rosa once ran a little bridal shop with a heart-shaped window. Now the doors are boarded up and someone powerful wants the land.

Merge threads, lace, flowers, cakes and jewellery into finer treasures, fill the order of every couple who walks in, and bring Rosa's Bridal back to life.

How to Play

  1. Tap a generator to craft new items on the board.
  2. Drag two matching items together to merge them into a better one.
  3. Deliver the items each couple orders to move the story forward and restore the shop.
