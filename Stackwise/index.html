About the Game

Pieces fall, you rotate and place them, and every full row disappears. Simple to learn, hard to put down.

Stackwise is a calm, classic brick game for quick breaks and long sessions alike, with smooth one-thumb controls, glossy neon blocks and four game modes.

Features

🎮

One-thumb controls

Drag to move, tap to rotate, flick down to drop. Buttons for hold, rotate and drop too.

⏱

Four modes

Marathon, Sprint (40 lines), Ultra (2 minutes) and a new Daily Challenge every day.

🔁

Hold and preview

Hold a piece for the perfect moment and plan ahead with the next-piece preview.

🎨

Five block themes

Neon, Pastel, Retro Pixel, Glass and Gold. Unlock them by playing.

💾

Never lose a game

Your game is saved automatically, so you can continue where you left off.

🌏

5 languages

English, Tiếng Việt, Deutsch, Español and Português (Brasil).

How to Play

  1. Drag left or right to move the falling piece; tap to rotate it.
  2. Fill a whole row to clear it. Clear several rows at once for more points.
  3. Keep the stack low as the pace rises, and beat your best score.
